# 7. Discussion and annotations

Every listed paper has an open discussion. Unlike formal reviews, discussion is open to any member, informal, and **never changes a paper's status**.

## Who can take part

- **Reading** discussions and annotations: anyone.
- **Posting, replying, annotating and voting**: tier 1 (Member) and above. See [Trust tiers](02-your-account.md#trust-tiers).

## Discussion threads

Open the **Discussion** tab on a paper, or `/discussion/{paper id}`.

- Start a thread or reply to any post. Replies can nest.
- Posts can be up to 4,000 characters.
- Choose **Sign and submit** to post. Every post is a signed record, like everything else on Scholium.
- Posts cannot be edited or deleted once submitted. Write with care.

### Helpful votes

You can mark another member's post as helpful, once per post. You cannot vote on your own posts, and you cannot withdraw a vote.

Helpful votes **only change the order** of the thread: the most helpful posts come first, then the oldest. Votes are never shown as counts on papers and never affect a paper's status.

## Annotations

An annotation is a comment attached to one paragraph of one version of a paper.

**To add one:**

1. In the reading view, click a paragraph, or move to it with Tab.
2. Press **A**.
3. Write your note and choose **Save annotation**. Escape closes the box without saving.

Annotations appear as markers beside the paragraph, and in the Discussion tab. Select a marker to open that paragraph's annotations under it; select it again to close them. If a paper has none yet, you will see "No annotations yet — select a paragraph and press A to add the first."

**Things to know:**

- An annotation attaches to the **whole paragraph**. You cannot select part of a sentence.
- Annotations belong to **one version**. A new version of the paper starts without annotations.
- Annotations a moderator has hidden show "Hidden by moderation."

## Report a post

Every post has a **⚑ Report** action. See [Reporting content](09-moderation-appeals-and-reports.md#reporting-content).

## Limits

Posts and votes count toward your [daily write limit](02-your-account.md#daily-limits). When you reach it you will see "You are going too fast. Try again in …".
