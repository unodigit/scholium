# 8. AI pre-review

Every listed paper gets an automated pre-review written by a team of AI agents. It is meant to help authors and reviewers spot problems early. It is **advisory only**.

> "Advisory. Generated by AI agents. Not a peer review."
>
> This banner sits on every AI report. The AI pre-review **cannot** change a paper's status, listing or anyone's standing. There is no path in the software from AI output to paper status.

AI content is always shown in its own purple colour, which is never used for human reviews.

## When it runs

The pre-review starts automatically when a version is **listed** by a moderator. Unlisted drafts are never sent for AI review. Until the report is ready, the **AI** tab says "This paper has no AI pre-review yet. One is written after a version is listed."

## The agents

| Agent | Looks at |
| --- | --- |
| Methodology | Study design, statistics and whether the method supports the claims |
| Literature | References and how the work relates to prior work |
| Integrity | Signs that need a human check, such as possible reuse of figures or text |
| Reproducibility | Runs the paper's code bundle, if there is one, in a locked-down sandbox and reads the result |
| Meta-review | Combines the findings into one report. This step is fixed code, not an AI model. |

## How to read a finding

Each finding shows:

- the **agent** that raised it;
- its **severity**: info, minor, major or critical;
- its **confidence**: low, medium or high;
- its **evidence**: a quotation from the paper, a numbered reference, or a line from the reproduction log. You can follow it to the source.

Findings without checkable evidence are dropped before the report is published.

Integrity findings are worded carefully. They end with "Needs a human check." The report never says "fraud" or "plagiarism detected". Any such wording from a model is replaced with "Possible integrity concern in the quoted text."

## Responding to findings

| Who | Action | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authors of the paper (tier 2+) | **Rebut**: explain why a finding is wrong or already addressed | The rebuttal appears next to the finding |
| Reviewers (tier 3) | **Confirm**: the finding is right | "Confirmed by a reviewer" |
| Reviewers (tier 3) | **Wrong**: the finding is mistaken | "Marked wrong by a reviewer" |

Only an author of the paper can rebut. Anyone else sees "Only a paper author may rebut an AI finding."

## The reproduction sandbox

If a paper includes code, the reproducibility agent runs it in an isolated sandbox:

- no network access;
- read-only system files;
- 1 CPU, 2 GB of memory and a 10-minute time limit.

If the server has no sandbox configured, the report says the reproduction was not run.

> **For node operators:** the AI provider is chosen with `AI_PROVIDER`: `anthropic`, `openai-compatible` or `ollama` (self-hosted). The sandbox backend is chosen with `SANDBOX_BACKEND`. Hosted providers refuse unlisted drafts. See `docs/deploy.md`.
